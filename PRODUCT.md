## Users

SIMKM is used by BPS Kabupaten Kepulauan Seribu staff across operational roles: administrators who manage accounts, roles, teams, and regions, and who also monitor team progress, survey execution, regional coverage, reports, and mitra performance across the whole organization; Pegawai BPS who manage surveys, assignments, partner activity, and data entry validation within their own teams; and mitra who need clear visibility into assigned survey work and personal progress.

Users open the system in an office or field coordination context where they need quick status checks, reliable data, and efficient follow-up without decorative friction.

## Product Purpose

SIMKM is a monitoring and management system for BPS activities and mitra performance. It exists to help BPS teams coordinate survey work, assign and track mitra responsibilities, validate entries, monitor operational progress, and produce reporting views for leadership.

Success means users can understand current performance, spot pending work, act on assignments, and move between role-specific workflows with confidence and minimal ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

The product should feel resmi, tenang, and efisien while still being pleasant to open and use. It should communicate institutional trust and operational clarity, with enough visual energy to keep dashboards readable and engaging during repeated use.

The interface should use BPS-aligned blue and white as the dominant palette, with orange and green as controlled supporting colors for emphasis, status, and secondary cues. It may be more colorful when the information benefits from it, but the overall impression must stay organized and restrained.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup SaaS styling, overly playful visuals, crowded dashboards, loud gradients, excessive decoration, and interfaces that feel detached from a government statistical institution.

Also avoid the opposite failure: flat, monotonous blue-white screens with weak hierarchy, low-contrast gray text, or dense tables that make monitoring feel tiring.

## Design Principles

- Put monitoring first: key status, progress, and exceptions should be visible before decoration.
- Use institutional color with discipline: blue and white carry identity, orange and green clarify meaning.
- Keep role workflows distinct: each user type should see the actions and signals most relevant to their responsibility.
- Make dense information scannable: tables, cards, charts, and filters should support quick comparison.
- Favor calm confidence: visual polish should increase trust and usability, not compete with the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special accessibility target is required beyond a strong baseline. Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable Indonesian UI copy, color-independent status indicators, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated behavior.
